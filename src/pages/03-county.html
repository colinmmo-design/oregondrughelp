What a BHRN can help with

Behavioral Health Resource Networks were set up in 2021 so that anyone struggling with drugs or alcohol has somewhere local to go. Each network is a group of agencies in your county that work together. You can walk in, call, or be referred by someone else.

  • Screening and referral: a conversation about what’s going on and what kind of help fits
  • A personal plan built with you, not for you
  • Low-barrier treatment that’s easy to start
  • Harm reduction, such as naloxone and safer-use supplies
  • Peer support from people with their own recovery experience
  • Employment support to help you get or keep work

Three ways to find your county’s network

  • Search the state directory. The Oregon Health Authority’s BHRN directory (PDF) lists every agency by county, with phone numbers.
  • Dial 211. Ask for the Behavioral Health Resource Network in your county.
  • Call 1-800-923-4357. The Oregon Alcohol & Drug Helpline can connect you, 24/7.
