About Oregon Drug Help

An independent, plain-language guide to getting help with drugs and alcohol in Oregon.

Last reviewed November 3, 2026 · Sources listed at the bottom of this page

Why this site exists

When you or someone you love is struggling with drugs or alcohol, finding real help is often confusing. Search results are crowded with ads and paid listings for treatment centers. Oregon Drug Help gathers the free, trustworthy options in one place and explains them in plain language.

The site is for anyone in Oregon: people worried about their own use, families and friends, and people in recovery.

Independent

Oregon Drug Help is not a treatment center and doesn’t refer people to any particular program. We’re not affiliated with any government agency, helpline or provider we mention.

How we check information

  • Official sources first. We rely on the Oregon Health Authority, county health departments, SAMHSA, Oregon law and established nonprofits such as Lines for Life.
  • Every page lists its sources at the bottom, so you can check them yourself.
  • Every page shows the date it was last reviewed. Phone numbers are checked against the organization’s own website.
  • Plain language. We explain medical and legal terms instead of assuming you know them.

What this site is not

This site gives general information. It isn’t medical or legal advice and can’t replace a conversation with a doctor, counselor or lawyer. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. In a crisis, call or text 988.
